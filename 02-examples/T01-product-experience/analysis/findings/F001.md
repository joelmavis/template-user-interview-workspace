# F001 — Starting point is hard to discover

**Illustrative example finding**

## Detailed explanation
The first step was not obvious in three different ways. Alex opened three menus before trying an action; Emma said a visible next step would keep her from leaving the task; Sam found the tool but hesitated over its wording. These accounts support an orientation problem, while describing different points of friction. Jordan's preference for a short guided start is related context, but is not counted as direct evidence for this finding.

## Supporting evidence
E001 (Alex, I001), E002 (Emma, I002), and E003 (Sam, I003). Their attributed words are preserved in the interview records.

## Interpretation and potential impact
A named first action could reduce uncertainty before use. This is an interpretation, not a measured effect. The four sessions are synthetic, contain short source notes, and provide no task-completion or population data.
