# F002 — Users want a concise guided first step

**Illustrative example finding**

## Detailed explanation
Emma wanted a visible next step so she would not leave the task for later. Jordan preferred a short guided start followed by freedom to explore. Both point toward light initial guidance, but they do not specify the same interface: one emphasizes visibility and the other a brief, optional sequence.

## Supporting evidence
E002 (Emma, I002) and E004 (Jordan, I004). The interview records preserve their attributed statements.

## Interpretation and potential impact
An optional, skippable guide is one possible response to these preferences. It remains a proposal; no session tested that design. The synthetic sample and short notes cannot establish how widely this need applies.
