Detailed Findings

F001 · Starting point is hard to discover

Detailed explanation. The first step was not obvious in three different ways. Alex opened three menus before trying an action; Emma said a visible next step would keep her from leaving the task; Sam found the tool but hesitated over its wording. These accounts support an orientation problem, while describing different points of friction. Jordan's preference for a short guided start is related context, but is not counted as direct evidence for this finding.

F002 · Users want a concise guided first step

Detailed explanation. Emma wanted a visible next step so she would not leave the task for later. Jordan preferred a short guided start followed by freedom to explore. Both point toward light initial guidance, but they do not specify the same interface: one emphasizes visibility and the other a brief, optional sequence.

Recorded evidence.

Original interview note

Recorded evidence · E004 · Jordan Kim · 14 September 2026, 10:00 UTC+0800

Original interview note

Interpretation and potential impact. An optional, skippable guide is one possible response to these preferences. It remains a proposal; no session tested that design. The sample and short notes cannot establish how widely this need applies.

Limit. Preference varied by experience level. · Status. reviewed

Finding source

Patterns and differences

The shared theme is orientation, not an identical problem for every person. Alex described navigation, Sam wording, Emma task continuity, and Jordan the desired amount of guidance. Jordan did not say they were blocked, so that statement should not be treated as another instance of failed discovery.

Opportunities and priorities

Would a clearly named first action help more people begin a task without adding guidance they do not want? Test the proposed designs with additional real participants if this approach is adopted.

Proposed solutions and action plan

A001 · Make the first task explicit

Proposed solution. Add a concise start panel that names the first useful action and allows dismissal.

Rationale and evidence. This addresses F001; the recorded statements and limits appear above. A concise start panel was simulated after approval. Rationale: F001. Validation is V001; it is not a production outcome.
