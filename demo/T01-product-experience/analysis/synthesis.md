# Product Experience Research — Synthesis

**Synthetic Demo** · Updated 2026-10-20

## Executive summary
Four synthetic interviews describe different aspects of getting started. Alex navigated three menus, Emma wanted a visible next step, Sam hesitated over wording, and Jordan preferred a brief guided start followed by exploration. F001 addresses discovery of the first action; F002 addresses the form of optional guidance. A001 has one synthetic follow-up result; A002 is still unapproved.

## Patterns and differences
The shared theme is orientation, not an identical problem for every person. Alex described navigation, Sam wording, Emma task continuity, and Jordan the desired amount of guidance. Jordan did not say they were blocked, so that statement should not be treated as another instance of failed discovery.

## Next research question
Would a clearly named first action help more people begin a task without adding guidance they do not want? Test the proposed designs with additional real participants if this approach is adopted.

## Limitations
These are four brief synthetic notes. No transcript, task timing, or population sample is available. The single V001 follow-up is illustrative and cannot establish general improvement. This material must never be presented as real user research.
